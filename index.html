Long (~120 words)

Thomas Prommer is a technology executive and AI-native operator based in New York. He is the founder and CEO of We The Flywheel, an AI-native digital group company he started in 2019 around a simple idea: agent-led execution, human strategy. Its operating companies span media, sports, product studio, enterprise AI advisory and personal-brand acceleration. Before founding We The Flywheel, Thomas was Global SVP of Engineering at adidas and CIO of Sweetgreen, and he has advised Bain Capital. His operating thesis is production-first, human-in-the-loop and measured by outcomes, not demos. Thomas is also a hybrid athlete who races Ironman and HYROX, and he brings the same discipline, systems thinking and consistency from training into how he builds and runs companies.
